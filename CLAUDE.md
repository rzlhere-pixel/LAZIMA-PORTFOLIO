## Non-negotiable rules
- Preserve original human photos **exactly** where identity preservation is requested. Do not generate a lookalike; do not alter face shape, skin, pose, body proportions, scarf, hair, hands, or clothing. Use compositing with original pixels, separate masks/layers, and 100%-zoom QA.
- Do not manufacture project images, original drawings, endorsements, titles, dates, metrics, client names, or employer permissions.
- Differentiate original student work, personal experiments, and professional employer contributions. Never claim ownership of a collaborator's or employer's collection.
- Treat sample CV language, draft interview answers, template images, and previous assistant outputs as **unverified** unless supported by original evidence.
- Keep personal contacts and private documents outside this public repo. Publish only after explicit subject/owner review and appropriate rights clearance.
- Do not upload original portraits, private employment records, resumes, internal trackers, images with third parties, contracts, or raw handoff archives until repository visibility is changed to **private** and each file has been reviewed.
- Exact brand lettering needs the approved wordmark source. Never replace it with a guessed cursive font while claiming exactness.
- No deployment, employer contact, LinkedIn update, public portfolio publication, or third-party permissions request without approval.
