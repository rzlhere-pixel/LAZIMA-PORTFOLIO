# LAZIQUE — Creative & Image Integrity Standards

## Primary visual motive

**Larger sketches, richer moodboards, softer colours, bold typography and minimal text.** Original fashion illustrations and creative development should dominate the page. Avoid template-heavy corporate presentation. See [VISUAL_DIRECTION.md](VISUAL_DIRECTION.md) for detailed art direction and [the prototype](../preview/index.html).

## Identity
Personal creative studio visual direction: **Quiet Luxury × Fashion Editorial × Personal Creative Studio**. Use restrained layering, believable shadows and elegant typography, not synthetic glamour or generic AI décor.

- Brand spelling: `lazique` (lowercase)
- Approved script/wordmark: **must be supplied as original editable image/vector source** before any exact matching claim.
- Tagline wording used in prior approved directions: `Uniquely Lazi, effortlessly chic.` (confirm capitalization against the chosen master artwork).
- Never silently bring back rejected background slogans or make up extra text.

## Human-image preservation
The photographic source controls subject identity. Preserve face, eyes, expression, visible skin texture, hair/headscarf, body, posture, garments, hands and fine details. No smoothing, facial reconstruction, generated replacement person, or crop that changes features. Any background generation must happen **separately** and be composited behind the untouched subject layer.

QA with source overlay and pixel comparison in the subject region. If a method changes the subject, disclose that exact limitation instead of claiming 100% preservation.

## Windows 11 wallpaper outputs
- Master `3840 × 2160`, landscape 16:9, sRGB PNG.
- Preview `1920 × 1080`, landscape 16:9, sRGB PNG.
- Keep approximately the left 26–30% quiet for desktop icons when requested.
- Keep title and face outside the Windows taskbar, desktop-icon zone and edge crop areas.
- Use original letterform graphic as an independent overlay; no AI-generated pseudo-typography.
- Never represent a reference collage or phone screenshot as a clean original portrait.

## Production
Retain original photographs separately, document selection/masking/placement, preserve source dimensions and checksum, store layered edits, then export derivative PNGs. Verify at 100% magnification; keep honest QC notes. Do not upload any of these originals to a public repository.
