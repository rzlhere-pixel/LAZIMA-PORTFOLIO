# LAZIMA — Visual-First Fashion Portfolio

**Status:** Owner-approved creative motive · 10 November 2026  
**Key words:** Expressive · tactile · editorial · soft · sketch-led · personal

> **Larger sketches. Richer moodboards. Softer colours. Bold typography. Minimal text.**

## 1. What the viewer should feel
A designer's sketchbook has become a polished fashion editorial. The illustrations, fabric research, shape studies and modest-wear concepts command attention. This is an **art-led portfolio**, not a commercial proposal, CV layout, graduation slideshow or text-heavy PDF.

## 2. Visual hierarchy
1. **Original artwork first:** full-height or unusually large fashion sketches; no artificial recreation.
2. **Mood second:** varied-scale moodboards with swatches, texture, material, silhouette and atmosphere.
3. **Editorial typography third:** oversized, confident headings contrast with tiny, intentional captions.
4. **Words last:** short titles and factual one-line captions rather than explanatory paragraphs.

## 3. Editorial palette

| Colour | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Milk ivory | `#F6F2EC` | Main page paper |
| Powder rose | `#E9D9D6` | Warm fashion mood |
| Dusty lilac | `#CFC7D7` | Soft concept section |
| Muted sage | `#C4CBBE` | Organic/fabric study |
| Misty blue | `#D5DFE5` | Calm accent |
| Deep plum | `#312734` | Type/contrast |
| Warm cocoa | `#756257` | Fine annotations |

Use two or three hues per spread, not every swatch simultaneously. Maintain legible contrast and accurate colours for garment photos.

## 4. Layout recipes
- **Hero sketch:** single high-quality illustration, 65–85% of page height, tiny lower-caption, very bold title.
- **Moodboard:** 5–8 authentic items at unequal scale; one dominant fabric/silhouette shot, two or three smaller images, a restrained row of sampled colours.
- **Study:** three sequential true hand sketches, with generous gaps and one-word directional notes.
- **Modest wear:** original fashion drawing paired with confirmed finished garment or true sample, textile/trim close-up and one succinct contribution line.
- **Construction:** technical flats, fitting notes and correct real measurements displayed legibly; avoid falsifying dimensions.
- **Finale:** one evocative image and an approved public contact only.

## 5. Type
Use a large high-contrast fashion-editorial serif for headings; a contemporary simple sans for sparse labels. Use only licensed/available fonts in the final Canva design. Headings should be genuinely large; supporting text 9–11pt in A4 print unless a technical note requires more. Keep labels consistent. Brand-specific `lazique` script must use the supplied approved wordmark, never an approximation.

## 6. What to avoid
Dense paragraphs, text cards filling every grid cell, generic AI fashion figures, invented swatch/photos, "premium" gradients that overpower the art, repeated stock models, illegible hairline typography, excessive gold/glitter and fabricated process achievements.

## 7. Assets required
Original pencil and digital fashion illustrations; original moodboards; proper garment photos; real textile and embellishment close-ups; sketches/technical flats; title/role/date per project; original-camera portraits only where permitted; author/photographer/employer credit and rights status. Recover missing originals before declaring anything complete.

## 8. Production and QC
Master is **A4 210 × 297 mm in Canva**, with editable text, images and shapes. Test the page in actual A4 print size and on a phone. Use no flattened-only deck as the sole deliverable. Do not invent source projects, treat illustrative placeholders as original, or claim photo identity is untouched without a source-pixel comparison.
